# Pass limits belong to destinations, with no limit on the total

A teacher told us they want only one student in the restroom at a time, with a
line, while students at the nurse or the counselor are never held up — and
they still want every trip recorded. One Pass Limit shared by every destination
could not do that, so each destination now carries its own Pass Limit (or
none), each limited destination has its own Line, and there is no longer any
limit on how many students may be out in total. We chose separate limits and
lines over a simpler "this destination uses the shared limit" switch because a
Restroom and a Water Fountain each limited to one should not take turns with
each other.

## Consequences

- Teachers who had one overall limit had it copied onto every destination,
  which can let more students out at once than before. Returning teachers are
  shown a one-time "What's changed" note pointing them to their destinations.
- Getting past a rule is recorded as its own kind of permission — an Extra Pass
  for the Pass Allowance, a No-Pass Exception for No-Pass Time, a Line Skip for
  a full destination — even though the teacher grants whichever are needed
  with a single "let them go".
